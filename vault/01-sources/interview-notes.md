# Interview Notes

## Research Context

- Project: Warehouse & Smart Inventory Management
- Research objective: Understand how minimart manager/staff currently perform Receive, Putaway, Pick, Transfer, Adjust, and Audit activities.
- Research method: P1 self-report / mini-interview; P2 and P3 written confirmation of summarized workflow.
- Participants: P1, P2, and P3. Real participant names are not stored in the Vault.
- Limitation: P1, P2, and P3 all work at the same minimart, so this evidence describes that minimart's current operation and must not be generalized to all warehouses.
- Evidence status: Human-confirmed research evidence provided for source ingestion. Unsupported details remain `TBD` or `OPEN QUESTION`.

## P1 - Quản lý minimart

- Participant ID: P1
- Role: Quản lý minimart
- Method: Self-report / mini-interview
- Current tool: KiotViet

### Receive

- When goods arrive, P1 checks the correct item and counts the actual received quantity.
- Actual received quantity is compared with expected quantity.
- If the delivery is complete, the received quantity is updated in KiotViet.
- If there is a discrepancy, it is handled with the delivery party.
- If the actual delivery is short or discrepant, only the actual received quantity is entered into the system, not the expected quantity.

### Putaway / Physical Storage

- The minimart has a backroom storage area.
- The minimart has a sales shelf area.
- After receiving, goods may be stored in the backroom or moved to the sales shelf.
- Current physical location knowledge mainly depends on the actual arrangement and the operator's experience.

### Pick

- Inventory quantity is tracked in KiotViet.
- Knowing where goods are located in the backroom/shelf area mainly depends on physical arrangement and staff experience.

### Transfer / Movement

- Current operation includes moving goods between the backroom storage area and the sales shelf area.
- It is not confirmed whether these internal movements are recorded as separate transactions in KiotViet.

### Adjust

- When actual stock differs from system data, the discrepancy must be checked again before adjustment handling.
- P1, as the manager, participates in checking/handling stock discrepancies.
- It is not confirmed whether ordinary staff can directly adjust stock in KiotViet.

### Audit

- The minimart performs inventory checking daily.
- Inventory checking includes checking/counting physical goods and comparing them with inventory data in KiotViet.
- When a discrepancy is found, it must be checked again before handling.

### Unresolved Questions

- Whether ordinary staff can directly adjust stock in KiotViet remains `OPEN QUESTION`.
- Whether internal movement between backroom and shelf is recorded as a separate transaction remains `OPEN QUESTION`.
- Detailed business rules for Receive, Putaway, Pick, Transfer, Adjust, and Audit remain `OPEN QUESTION`.
